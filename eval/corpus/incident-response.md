# Incident response runbook

When a service outage is detected, the on-call engineer acknowledges the alert within five minutes and opens an incident channel.

Severity 1 incidents affect all customers and require a status page update every thirty minutes. Severity 2 incidents affect a subset of customers and require an update every two hours.

After resolution, the incident owner writes a blameless postmortem within five working days and shares it with the whole engineering team.
