# Backup policy

Nightly backups of the shared drive run at 02:00 and are kept for 35 days. Monthly snapshots are kept for twelve months and then deleted automatically.

Restoring a deleted file requires a ticket to the infrastructure team. Restores of files younger than seven days are completed within one business day.

Laptops are not backed up. Employees must store work documents on the shared drive instead of the local disk.
